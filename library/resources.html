  • ~~FedRAMP level for GCC: Microsoft sources conflict (Moderate vs High).~~ Resolved by [S35]: GCC's formal P-ATO is FedRAMP Moderate (from DHHS), but Microsoft has completed FedRAMP High Impact Level audits (Security Assessment Reports) in GCC and advertises FedRAMP High "Equivalency" for Federal Agency ATO purposes. GCC High carries Agency ATOs at both Moderate and High Impact Level (DHS, DoJ, FBI, Treasury). [S5]'s "FedRAMP Moderate" and [S8]'s "FedRAMP High" were both accurate, describing different accreditation instruments for the same environment.
