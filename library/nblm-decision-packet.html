Executive Decision Packet: Microsoft Purview Architecture & Investment Strategy

1. First 60 seconds (spoken script)

We recommend the immediate authorization and deployment of Microsoft Purview Data Security Posture Management (DSPM) for AI and the Unified Catalog within a dedicated Data Management Landing Zone (DMLZ), aligned with Microsoft’s Cloud Adoption Framework.

Today, 78% of enterprise users bring unsanctioned consumer AI tools to work, creating immediate data exfiltration pathways. Beyond traditional search, agentic AI introduces a step-function increase in risk: autonomous agents reason over unlabelled, overshared content—such as confidential M&A files in our SharePoint environment—and can surface sensitive enterprise assets to unauthorized or unauthenticated external users via Copilot Studio extensions.

To protect our data estate and enable secure AI adoption, we require executive committee approval today to establish the dedicated governance landing zone, allocate platform capacity, and approve the 90-day implementation roadmap.

If we do nothing, UNKNOWN - will be known by 2026-10-15 via CISO Risk Assessment / Financial Exposure Analysis. If we act, unified AI data security and oversharing protection will be operational by 2026-12-31 at UNKNOWN - will be known by 2026-10-15 via Tenant SKU Audit.

3. Five killer questions (20-second answers)

1. Why now?

Spoken Answer: 78% of users bring unsanctioned consumer AI tools to work, while AI agents reason over overshared, unlabelled SharePoint data without oversight. Deploying DSPM for AI gives us immediate visibility across AI interactions, applies risk-adaptive guardrails, and enforces the necessary Collection Policies required to capture prompt and response telemetry for Fabric and Security Copilot.

  • Elevate: Ungoverned AI expansion expands the enterprise attack surface daily, turning latent permission drift into automated public data loss. (Sources: [S15], [S28])

2. What if we wait? (dated cost of no)

Spoken Answer: Delaying deployment allows unmonitored data exfiltration to consumer LLMs like ChatGPT and DeepSeek to continue unchecked. Autonomous AI agents will continue to reason over unprotected confidential business files—such as Project Obsidian M&A data—exposing high-value enterprise assets to unauthorized users.

  • Elevate: Inaction guarantees ongoing permission drift, unmonitored agent grounding, and unmitigated regulatory non-compliance. (Sources: [S17], [S28])

3. Who else has done this?

Spoken Answer: This deployment directly executes Microsoft’s Cloud Adoption Framework reference architecture for Unified Data Platforms, deploying Purview within a dedicated Data Management Landing Zone per Entra tenant.

  • Elevate: Leveraging Microsoft’s standardized deployment topology ensures full platform supportability and eliminates fragile, custom governance architectures; third-party peer benchmarks remain unverified in source documents. (Source: [S24])

4. What are we stopping? (capacity / tradeoff)

Spoken Answer: We are halting fragmented, application-by-application security administration, manual data cataloging workflows, and reliance on disparate third-party classification tools.

  • Elevate: Eliminating custom point-solution management reallocates operational capacity toward automated AI security posture management and continuous threat prevention. (Sources: [S24], [S28])

5. What if this slips? (risk and recovery)

Spoken Answer: If deployment slips, multi-cloud platforms like AWS S3 and Snowflake will remain unmonitored by DSPM. Furthermore, in Azure Government environments, AWS S3 scanning remains a hard coverage gap due to connector exclusions, requiring commercial Purview account bridging or fallback manual access reviews.

  • Elevate: Schedule slippage leaves multi-cloud AI data flows unprotected, increasing operational reliance on labor-intensive manual audits. (Sources: [S1], [S4], [S9], [S10], [S16])
